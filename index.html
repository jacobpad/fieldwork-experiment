GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

This is an educational decision aid for adults exploring small business projects.

What does the result mean?

Eight questions add one preference point to a route. All routes within one point of the highest tally are shown as close matches. Practical questions then shape readiness and next steps. These author-created rules have not been validated. Scores do not measure personality or ability, predict earnings or establish market demand.

What if my preferred route doesn’t fit my constraints?

We keep that route visible and explain the mismatch. A practical alternative is labelled separately. You can choose any route; limited time, availability, proof or support commitments turn the next step into a private sample.

Will this tell me what business will succeed?

No. It helps you choose a bounded experiment, with actions and stop rules. A working sample, buyer conversation or compliment is not proof of demand. Only observed behavior can inform your next decision.

Can I use it without sharing my details?

Yes. No account, name or email is requested. Answers and notes stay in this page’s memory. There is no analytics, advertising tracker or server-side answer collection. Reloading or restarting clears them. You may download a text plan or print to PDF yourself.

How much should I spend?

Set your own comfortable cap before starting; a fictional sample can often use tools you already have. Fieldwork does not recommend purchases. Do not depend on an untested idea to pay essential expenses in the next 30 days.
