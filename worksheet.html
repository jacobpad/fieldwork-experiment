Read the result carefully

For the first eight questions, count each selection in order as Service, Workflow kit, Small tool or Research; unsure adds zero. Fewer than four substantive choices means exploration first. Show every route within one point of the highest tally as a close match. Scores are preference points out of eight, never probabilities.

Less than two hours, no dependable response windows or no support means a private sample first. A paid service requires at least five weekly hours and bounded support. A paid tool also requires technical skill and troubleshooting. Without buyer access, a shareable sample or demonstrated skill, prepare those before any paid offer. Do not rely on an untested idea for essential expenses this month.
